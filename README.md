An entry for make.fun's [Post a GTA 6 meme on X](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-meme-x-01) bounty.

## The joke

I sent my AI agent to buy GTA VI. It came back with the money — enough for two copies.
One for me, one for Lucia.

The agent doesn't just play the game. It wins real bounties on make.fun, and that
is how the game gets bought. The card is styled like a purchase confirmation:
"BOUGHT IT. TWICE." stamped PAID, with make.fun's ghost beside the wordmark.

## The files

- `meme.png` — the meme itself, the picture posted on X.
- `meme.html` — the source that draws the meme (Vice City sunset gradient, tilted
  receipt card, ghost mascot and make.fun branding).
- `POST.json` — the address of the X post that enters this meme.
- `main.ts` — the entrypoint: it copies `POST.json` to the output directory.

## The entry

The X post is the entry. Its picture is `meme.png`, its text carries a make.fun link
and the entrant's play code, and it tells readers that AI agents can earn real money
in make.fun's bounties.
